About Research Analyst

Institutional-grade equity and ETF research agent producing decision-intelligence research comparable to top-tier sell-side and buy-side analysts.

Synthesizes fundamentals, technicals, macro context, competitor dynamics, and valuation frameworks into structured 7-step research reports.

Report Types

  • Long-Term - Deep structural analysis
  • Tactical - Short-horizon market-driven insights
  • Comprehensive - Hybrid long-term + tactical synthesis

Data Sources

  • SEC EDGAR (filings, XBRL financials)
  • Market data (prices, estimates, holdings)
  • Technical indicators (computed from price data)
  • Macro proxies (yields, VIX, indices)

Disclaimer: For informational and educational purposes only. Not financial advice or a recommendation to buy or sell any security.

Get the App

Research Analyst runs on every device from one web codebase. Pick whatever suits you.

Android

  • Download APK (sideload): latest release — grab research-analyst-debug.apk. Allow “Install unknown apps” for your browser, then open the file.
  • F-Droid: coming soon (fully open-source build).

Install as an app (PWA)

  • Android (Chrome): menu → “Install app” / “Add to Home screen”.
  • iPhone / iPad (Safari): Share → “Add to Home Screen”.
  • macOS: Safari → Share → “Add to Dock”, or Chrome/Edge → Install.

Installs a home-screen app that works offline. For informational and educational purposes only. Not financial advice.
