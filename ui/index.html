About Research Analyst

Research Analyst is an institutional-grade equity and ETF research agent that produces decision-intelligence research comparable to top-tier sell-side and buy-side analysts.

It synthesizes fundamentals, technicals, macro context, competitor dynamics, and valuation frameworks into clear, structured 7-step research reports.

Supported Report Types

  • Long-Term — Deep, structural, timeless analysis
  • Tactical — Short-horizon, market-driven insights
  • Comprehensive — Hybrid long-term + tactical synthesis

Data Sources

  • SEC EDGAR (filings, XBRL financials)
  • Market data (prices, estimates, institutional holdings)
  • Technical indicators (computed from price data)
  • Macro proxies (Treasury yields, VIX, market indices)
