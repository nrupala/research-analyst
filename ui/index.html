About Research Analyst

Institutional-grade equity and ETF research agent producing decision-intelligence research comparable to top-tier sell-side and buy-side analysts.

Synthesizes fundamentals, technicals, macro context, competitor dynamics, and valuation frameworks into structured 7-step research reports.

Report Types

  • Long-Term - Deep structural analysis
  • Tactical - Short-horizon market-driven insights
  • Comprehensive - Hybrid long-term + tactical synthesis

Data Sources

  • SEC EDGAR (filings, XBRL financials)
  • Market data (prices, estimates, holdings)
  • Technical indicators (computed from price data)
  • Macro proxies (yields, VIX, indices)
