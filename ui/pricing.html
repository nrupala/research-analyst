FAQ

What counts as an API call?

One request to a metered API endpoint (research, quant, comparison, resolution, and the other keyed routes). Calls the pipeline fails are never counted against your allowance.

What happens when I reach my monthly allowance?

Further calls are refused with the count you have used, until the month rolls over. GET /api/usage shows your plan, your usage, and what remains, any time.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime — email info@aimlds.org and we cancel the same business day. You keep paid access until the end of the billing period; after that the key stops working and the free site keeps working as before.

What am I not buying?

The underlying market data. Paid plans license Research Analyst’s own analysis. Source data is consumed under its own licences and is never sold, redistributed, or exported raw — including through the API and the export features.

Is this financial advice?

No. Research Analyst is a research and education tool. Nothing it produces is a recommendation to buy or sell any security.
